Emotions that leads me to find my passion to do Web Development

I want to discuss how it all started for me in taking the Web Development

1. Boredom

During the pandemic there was not much to do as everything shifts to online. I became bored at home with so little to do but so much time to spend at home so i think of how much online oriented the world will be after the pandemic then i think of web developing.

2. Curiousity

After a few days of being bored at home i became curious of how websites are made and how are they able to format it.

3. Dissapointment

After becoming curious i tried to replicate things involving web development that i saw in YouTube but to no avail i always failed or made an error.

4. Satisfaction

After many failed attempts of making a small webpage i finally overcame the failure when i was able to make something similar to a website from a YouTube video and finally being satisfied with my creation.

5. Happiness

After overcaming multiple attempts in Web Developing and being satisfied with my results i decided to challenge myself to learn more and improve my shortcomings Then it finally clicks on me that this is what i wanted to do and pursue.

Tips & Tools

March 2026 3 min read

IT tools and platforms that i discovered and help me as a Web Developer and Designer

Back at my first year of college i often make mistaakes and got confused but luckily for me i found some IT tools that helped me improve my fundamentals

1. Figma

This is a online website platform that i use to make frameworks for my website. The advantage of Figma is that it helps you make clear frameworks and possible animations so that you can have an idea how to develop and design your own website.

2. FreeCodeCamp

FreeCodeCamp is an online platform that can teach you tutorials regarding programming languages like python, Java, JavaScript, PHP and others. This is a very useful website as not only it can teach you beginner lesson but also advance and difficult lessons. Another advantage of FreeCodeCamp is its forum as you can ask questions, ask solutions, give advice, and others from other people that uses FreeCodeCamp may it be a fellow students or professionals.

3. W3SCHOOL

This is probably the most used platforms in learning the basics of Web Development but it earned its spot because it completely explained the basics of Web Development in a completely detailed and fun version. For Web Developers knowing the fundamentals is the most important thing because how can you advance if the fundamentals is challenging for you.
